Coach note

Think about it

High output, low rest and staying consistent all matter. That's the story of a wrestling match.

  1. What was your best round, your worst round, and the gap between them?
  2. Could you have added one more rep per round? What would that have done to your total?
  3. Did you ever start a round late or stop a second or two early?
  4. When you got tired, were you tempted to cheat your standards?
  5. How would you have done in a surprise one-minute overtime?
